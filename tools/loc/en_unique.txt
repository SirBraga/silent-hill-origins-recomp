0067	{01}{01}TIPS
0068	{01}{01}As Travis explores, he will find maps for the locations he visits in Silent Hill. These maps will be useful in your travels. Travis will make note of important items and doors on the map as he discovers them. Check your map if you get lost or aren't sure what to do next.
0069	{01}{01}Travis will turn his head to look at objects of interest. This will help you spot important items as you explore. Push {03}{01} when standing in front of objects of interest to interact with them.
0070	{01}{01}To attack an enemy, hold down {03}{03} to assume a combat stance. You will lock onto enemies when they are close enough. Press the left and right directional buttons to change targets while locked on.
0071	{01}{01}Once you inflict enough damage on a monster, it will collapse to the floor. If left alone, it will get back on its feet. To prevent this, you should finish it off as it writhes on the ground. To perform a finishing move, release the {03}{03} and press {03}{01} when in front of the fallen enemy.
0072	{01}{01}Move toward your target with the left analog stick and hit {03}{01} to perform a forward attack. Often, these attacks have greater reach or inflict more damage.
0073	{01}{01}Hold down {03}{01} while in the combat stance to charge up a high-powered attack. Release {03}{01} to attack targets in range. 
0074	{01}{01}There are many large, heavy items in Silent Hill that can be used as weapons. Items such as Typewriters or Portable TVs will break when used, but also cause a great deal of damage. Large items can also be thrown at distant enemies: hold {03}{01} while locked on to a target, then release to throw the item.
0075	{01}{01}The streets and buildings of Silent Hill are full of items that can be used as weapons. Wooden planks, pitchforks, and even firearms. To pick up a weapon, press {03}{01} when you are standing in front of it. You can then equip the weapon from the inventory screen (press {03}{07}). Alternatively, you can pick up and equip a weapon instantly by pressing the down directional button. The weapon will go straight into Travis's hands, ready for use.
0076	{01}{01}As Travis explores Silent Hill, he will discover what appear to be two overlapping versions of the town: the deserted streets of the Misty World, and the dark, dangerous nightmare that is the Other World. Travis can use mirrors to move between these realities. Stand in front of a mirror and press {03}{01} to phase into the other world. Mirrors work both directions and can be used an unlimited number of times.
0077	{01}{01}Travis loses energy as he runs, attacks, or after a grapple with a monster. If he loses too much energy, he will become exhausted. When exhausted, Travis will run slowly and will have a harder time grappling successfully. To restore stamina, Travis should rest for a while. You can also use energy drinks to replenish stamina and give Travis a momentary boost. 
0078	{01}{01}Some monsters will grab Travis if they can get close enough. During these grapples, Travis must act quickly to avoid taking damage. There are two types of grapple attacks, both requiring a different response. When you see an icon with a moving arrow over it you must hammer the button as fast as you can to repel the enemy. If there is no arrow and the button is pulsing, you have a limited time to hit that button in order to defend against an attack.
0079	{01}{01}As Travis explores, he will collect many items of various shapes and sizes. These items will be stored in his inventory. Push {03}{07} to go to the inventory screen. You can cycle between item categories with {03}{04} and {03}{03}. Once you select an item, press {03}{01} to see the available actions. You can {01}{07}examine{01}{01} items for additional information that may be useful, or {01}{07}equip{01}{01} and {01}{07}reload{01}{01} your weapons. Some objects can be "used." If you wish to use an item, stand where you would like to use it and select the {01}{07}use{01}{01} option on the inventory screen. Many puzzles can be solved this way.
